Dataset Overview | National Centers for Environmental Information (NCEI)

Experimental Harmful Algal Bloom (HAB) Bulletin forecast products from Lake Erie from 2012-05-17 to 2012-10-12 created by the NOAA National Centers for Coastal Ocean Science (NCEI Accession 0242874)

Preview graphic
This archive information package contains outputs from the NOAA Harmful Algal Bloom (HAB) Experimental Forecast for Lake Erie in the form of bulletin documents beginning on 2012-05-17 and continuing to 2012-10-12. The HAB Experimental Bulletin was operated by the National Centers for Coastal Ocean Science to develop and produce experimental forecast bulletins for cyanobacteria blooms (blue-green algae) in Lake Erie. The bulletins contain an analysis of the location of cyanobacteria blooms, as well as 3-day forecasts of transport, mixing, scum formation and bloom decline based on the analysis of information from data providers. The bulletins were emailed to subscribers twice weekly during an active bloom. Each year, NCEI created a new accession containing the data collected during the previous 12 months. The experimental bulletin was started in 2008 and became operational in 2017. Note, from 2008-2013 the supporting imagery for the bulletins are not included in the archive packages due to inconsistent historical documentation during the development phase. In 2025, the National Centers for Coastal Ocean Science reprocessed the full Lake Erie satellite time-series (2000-present) to generate a consistent data product across three satellite missions: Medium Resolution Imaging Spectrometer (MERIS, 2002-2011), the Moderate Resolution Imaging Spectroradiometer (MODIS, 2000-2002; 2012-2018), and the Sentinel-3 Ocean and Land Colour Instrument (OLCI, 2016-Present; Mishra et al. 2026, DOI: 10.1088/2752-664X/ae4631). Following this re-processing, we now include 10-day composite satellite images of the cyanobacterial index (CI) for each bloom season from 2000-present. Previous time-series imagery can be found in accessions covering years from 2000-2007, titled Harmful Algal Bloom (HAB) Monitoring using satellite imagery for Lake Erie. Additional time-series imagery from 2017-present can be found in the Forecast products and associated satellite imagery from Lake Erie created by the NOAA Harmful Algal Bloom Operational Forecast System (HAB-OFS) collection.
